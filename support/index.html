Support

Email support@secretelectricity.studio.

Support is provided in English, during UK business hours. You can expect a first response within two business days.

What to include

A report with these details can usually be answered in one reply rather than three:

DetailWhy it helps
Your Jira site URLIdentifies the installation
Which app, and the app versionShown on the Manage apps screen
What you expected to happenSeparates a bug from a misunderstanding
What happened insteadThe exact error text, if any
Steps to reproduce itThe fastest route to a fix
A screenshotOften settles it immediately

Please do not include passwords, API tokens or personal data about your users. None of it is needed to diagnose a problem.

Before reporting a TypeLock problem

Two things explain most reports:

Nothing is being enforced

Check the workflow was published after the rule was added. A draft workflow has no effect. Open the Create transition and confirm the TypeLock rule is listed, then choose Update workflow.

Someone who should be allowed is blocked

Rules match on group and project role membership at the moment of creation. Confirm the user is actually in an allowed group, or holds an allowed role in that project. Administrators are not exempt, and neither is Jira Automation — see the TypeLock documentation.

Reporting a security issue

Email support@secretelectricity.studio with "security" in the subject. Reports are acknowledged promptly and taken seriously. Please give us a reasonable opportunity to address an issue before disclosing it publicly.

Feature requests

Welcome, and genuinely read. These apps are deliberately small and single-purpose, so the answer to "can it also do X" is often no — but knowing what you need shapes what gets built next.
