Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what Secret Electricity apps do with data. It applies to TypeLock for Jira and to every app we publish on the Atlassian Marketplace.

Who is responsible

These apps are provided by Secret Electricity ("we", "us"), not by Atlassian. We, and not Atlassian, are responsible for the privacy, security and integrity of any end user data processed by our apps. Questions, requests and complaints come to us at support@secretelectricity.studio.

What our apps collect

No personal data. We operate no database, no external service and no analytics, and we keep no record of any user.

TypeLock stores the settings of each rule an administrator creates. Those settings live on the Jira workflow, inside your own Jira site:

  • the ID and name of the restricted work item type
  • the IDs and names of the permitted groups
  • the IDs and names of the permitted project roles
  • the error message the administrator chose to display

This configuration is stored by Jira, in Jira. We never receive a copy. It describes groups and roles rather than individuals, and contains no names, email addresses or account identifiers of your users.

What our apps read

To let an administrator build a rule, the configuration screen reads the work item types, groups and project roles that exist on your site. That data is displayed in the form and is not stored, transmitted or retained once the screen closes.

When someone creates a work item, Jira evaluates the rule against that person's group and project role memberships. That evaluation happens inside Jira. The app does not see, receive or record the result, and no user identity ever reaches us.

TypeLock holds these read-only permissions, and no others:

PermissionPurpose
read:issue-type:jiraList work item types in the configuration screen
read:avatar:jira, read:project-category:jiraRequired by Jira's work item type endpoint
read:group:jiraList groups in the configuration screen
read:project-role:jira, read:project:jiraList project roles in the configuration screen

The app holds no write permission. It cannot create, modify or delete anything in your Jira site.

Where data is processed

Entirely within Atlassian's infrastructure. Our apps run on Atlassian Forge, Atlassian's hosted platform. They make no network requests to any third party, and no customer data leaves Atlassian's systems at any point.

What we do not collect

For the avoidance of doubt, our apps do not collect, store or transmit:

  • personal data of any kind
  • work item content, comments, attachments or field values
  • user names, email addresses, account IDs or profile data
  • usage analytics, telemetry or crash reports
  • IP addresses or device information

Nothing is sent to any AI or machine learning service.

Sub-processors

None. Because our apps transmit no data outside Atlassian's infrastructure, they engage no sub-processors. Atlassian acts as the infrastructure provider under its own terms with you.

Retention and deletion

The only data our apps cause to exist is the rule configuration described above, held by Jira on your workflow. It persists until an administrator deletes the rule or the workflow. Uninstalling an app leaves that configuration in Jira, where you control it; we hold no copy to delete.

Because we store no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain, export or erase on request. Requests concerning data held in your Jira site are addressed to Atlassian or to your site administrator.

Your rights under data protection law

Rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and comparable laws apply to personal data held by a controller or processor. We hold none in connection with these apps. If you believe otherwise, write to us and we will investigate and respond.

Security

Our apps request the minimum permissions they need, and prefer read-only access. TypeLock's enforcement logic runs as a Jira expression evaluated by Jira, not in a browser, so it cannot be bypassed by a modified client. There is no server of ours to breach and no stored credentials.

Vulnerability reports are welcome at support@secretelectricity.studio and will be acknowledged promptly.

Changes to this policy

Material changes appear here with a revised date. Where a change affects how data is handled, we will note it in the app's Marketplace listing.
